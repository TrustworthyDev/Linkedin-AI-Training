KP Oli Flappy is a lighthearted, unofficial remix of the classic Flappy Bird, inspired by Nepali internet culture. We've traded feathers for a Dhaka topi, and given a familiar face a little extra altitude.

No accounts. No downloads. No politics. Just a few pipes, a little pixel art, and an unreasonable number of "one more tries."

This is playful parody, not a political statement. Not affiliated with or endorsed by KP Sharma Oli. Your score and sound preference stay in your browser.
