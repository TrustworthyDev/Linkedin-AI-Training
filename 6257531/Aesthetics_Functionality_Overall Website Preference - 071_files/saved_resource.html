Get to know your market workspace.

Is this real-time market data?

By default, ticker runs a clearly labeled demo feed with simulated prices and historical charts. Open Settings and connect a Finnhub API key to receive live equity quotes. Available coverage and delays depend on your plan. Index benchmarks and sentiment remain illustrative.

How do I build my watchlist?

Use the plus button in your watchlist or press Ctrl K (Command K on Mac). Search by company or symbol, then click a star to save an asset. Click a saved stock in your watchlist to bring its chart into focus.

Where are my holdings and alerts saved?

Your watchlist, sample portfolio, preferences, and alerts are saved locally in this browser. Nothing is placed or traded. Price alerts work while the dashboard is open. API keys stay in memory for this session and are never saved to local storage.

Can I inspect and export the chart?

Hover over the chart, or focus it and use the left and right arrow keys, to inspect prices. Switch the range or chart style, or expand it for a closer view. Use Export data to download the current market snapshot as a CSV file. Live mode only charts quotes collected during this session.
