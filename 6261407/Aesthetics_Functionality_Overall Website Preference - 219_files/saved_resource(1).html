A fish is a surprisingly rich starting point. A curve can become a current. A little space can tell a whole story. Here's what ties our collection together.

01Simple paths. Strong silhouettes.

Every mark begins with a considered shape, not extra detail. Clean geometry and purposeful negative space keep each fish recognizable, from a tiny favicon to a big, bold sign.

02Two expressions. The same idea.

Flat for clarity. A subtle gradient for a little depth. Both versions share the same silhouette and a restrained palette of two or three colors, so your brand always feels like itself.

03Vector by nature. Ready to go.

Download editable SVGs with transparent backgrounds, or a high-resolution PNG for everyday use. All artwork is built from paths, circles, and curves. No fonts or embedded stock images are needed.
