How to review

Inspect Website A and Website B in their tabs, then answer all three question sets independently.

Aesthetics: Judge only visual quality: layout, composition, typography, color, polish, spacing, and visual artifacts. Ignore whether controls work.

Functionality: Judge only functionality and prompt-following behavior: whether the requested content renders and relevant links, buttons, controls, navigation, or interactive behavior work. Ignore visual polish unless broken rendering prevents evaluation.

Overall: Judge the complete experience after considering both aesthetics and functionality. Your overall choice does not need to match either independent choice.

If a website fails to load, freezes the browser tab, or cannot be rendered enough to inspect, treat that as a functionality failure. You may refresh the page to return to the Instructions tab.
