Talk to our team
Do you offer delivery?

Yes, delivery is one of our service options. Please call 2421 8663 or prepare an enquiry to confirm delivery areas, charges and timing before arranging your order.

Can I check if a product is in stock?

Of course. Call us with the product name, strength, pack size or any other details you have. Our team can confirm availability and the current price.

Where can I find Jackson Pharmacy?

Find us at Ground Floor, 78A, Tin On House, 78-96 Shek Yam Road, Kwai Chung. Our map reference is 949Q+RV Kwai Chung. Use the directions link below to plan your visit.

How do I arrange an order or delivery?

Call our team to discuss your needs. You can also use the enquiry form to prepare a summary, then copy it and share it when you call. Enquiries made here are not automatically sent or confirmed as orders.

Can I ask about a prescription or medical concern?

Please call us to discuss your specific needs and confirm what we can help with. This website is not a medical consultation service. For urgent or serious symptoms, seek professional medical care. In an emergency in Hong Kong, call 999.
