Brand guide

A considered identity.

Every element carries intent. Use the identity with the same care with which it was created.

Give the mark room to breathe.

Keep clear space equal to at least the height of the letter "a" around every edge. Always preserve the original proportions. Never stretch, skew, or add effects to the vector artwork.

A palette with purpose.

Pastel gold and antique brass bring warmth to gunmetal. Matte black and pure white provide essential, high-contrast alternatives. Select a color to copy its HEX value.

Choose the right signature.

The full signature is the primary expression. Use the wordmark for horizontal placements and the monogram for compact applications. Recommended starting widths: 40 mm for the signature, 30 mm for the wordmark, and 8 mm for the monogram.

Proof it on the material.

Fine strokes need consideration at engraving and embroidery scale. Test the mark with your fabricator, verify line thickness, and simplify only when production requires it. The SVG artwork contains no font dependencies.
